Source/build checks validate Python syntax/imports, parser construction, native
schema references and operation routing inventory without constructing Runtime or
opening stores. The new source records zero model, product, simulation and
benchmark executions. New-runtime verification is the next stage, not an inferred
success from the code's structure.

The $0 teacher connection needs explicit feedback import. It is not an automatic
ChatGPT subscription API, and its identity/usage are operator-declared. Paid hosted
teacher inference is not enabled. C5/C6 support only their existing mathematical
and evidence eligibility subsets. General perception, unsupported dynamics,
physical actuators, multi-user authentication, hard isolation, HA and weight
training are not added by connecting existing components.

Use [the configuration handoff](../../examples/integration/README.md) and fresh
workspaces. Original book/architecture snapshots, historical failures and the
frozen Meadows benchmark remain unchanged. Broader original contract reviews
remain REVISE; the scoped new connection contract is reviewed separately. No
scientific capability, advantage or complete original-architecture qualification
is claimed before testing.
