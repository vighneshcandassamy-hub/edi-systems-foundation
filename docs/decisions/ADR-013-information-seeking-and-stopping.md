Information requests may be justified by an information need. Claims about the
world still need evidence. This distinction does not grant a new operation or
choose a resource, hypothesis, task answer or investigation sequence.

Under the new policy, yield requires a nonblank reason and an assessment:
disposition (blocked, paused, no-useful-next-step), up to eight unresolved needs,
one to eight distinct advertised alternatives with reasons for declining them,
and a resumption condition. A blocked disposition must identify an unmet need.
Native admission rejects missing assessments or invented alternative names and
retains the original output through the existing bounded correction path.
The decoder requires the same yield envelope. Legacy proposal/controller
requests retain their old optional-yield shape. New policy inquiries require
C0 access and progress; a fresh C0 workspace is mandatory for older journals.

An assessment is a fallible student judgment, not proof of optimal stopping,
exhaustive search, evidence coverage, or task completion. No text classifier
attempts to certify the truth of its explanation. Deliberate stopping remains
possible before acquisition. There is no forced action, source count, teacher
cycle, or retry after an unknown dispatch. A successful public submission receipt
remains the software completion condition; that receipt does not prove correctness.

Teacher availability is explicit. An unconfigured teacher proposal returns
native correction feedback rather than an attempted missing dependency.
Configured teacher review stays model-selected; the teacher provides critique
and the student chooses its revised action. Teacher control has no promoted
access/progress or operation interface. Source text cannot change policy or grants.

Alternatives considered: prompt-only clarification had already failed live;
forcing a source/teacher would change the autonomous task; treating every yield
as a retry could consume the entire budget or suppress legitimate abstention.
The chosen contract adds bounded inspectable decisions, not a heuristic solver.
It may still fail with this checkpoint/model. Only a fresh pinned diagnostic can
measure whether information seeking improves. Existing acceptance is unchanged.
